---

## Exam Overview

The **BCI CBCI Certification** validates foundational knowledge and understanding of business continuity management practices. It covers the activities required to establish, implement, maintain, and validate a business continuity management system (BCMS).

- **Exam Format**: Online, closed-book, remotely proctored examination consisting of multiple-choice questions.
- **Target Audience**: Business continuity and organizational resilience professionals, aspiring practitioners, and professionals with business continuity, crisis management, or risk-related responsibilities.
- **Prerequisites**: No formal entry requirements are specified in the qualification specification. Completion of the full CBCI Certification Course is required to register for the online exam.

## Core Exam Domains & Knowledge Areas

The CBCI 7.0 qualification is based on six Professional Practices from the BCI Good Practice Guidelines (GPG) 7.0.

### 1. PP1 – Establishing a Business Continuity Management System

- Establishing, coordinating, and maintaining a business continuity management system.
- Defining BCMS scope, stakeholder involvement, and business continuity policy.
- Establishing governance, management support, roles, and responsibilities.

### 2. PP2 – Embracing Business Continuity

- Understanding organizational culture and the difference between embracing and embedding business continuity.
- Developing awareness, commitment, and a positive business continuity culture.
- Measuring business continuity culture and maintaining stakeholder engagement.

### 3. PP3 – Analysis

- Applying Business Impact Analysis (BIA) and Risk Assessment (RA).
- Identifying business impacts, dependencies, and critical activities.
- Determining Maximum Tolerable Period of Disruption (MTPD) and Recovery Time Objectives (RTO).

### 4. PP4 – Solutions Design

- Developing business continuity strategies and recovery solutions.
- Selecting solutions based on resources, recovery requirements, and organizational needs.
- Addressing unacceptable risks, vulnerabilities, and single points of failure.

### 5. PP5 – Enabling Solutions

- Implementing business continuity solutions and establishing response structures.
- Managing incident communications and response procedures.
- Developing and maintaining strategic, tactical, and operational business continuity plans.

### 6. PP6 – Validation

- Understanding the purpose and importance of business continuity validation.
- Developing, conducting, and evaluating business continuity exercises.
- Maintaining, reviewing, and improving the business continuity management system.
